# Private provisional per-overload ABI schema inventory

This scratch revision extends the existing private 15-record ABI inventory with 80 ordered provisional records. Each has an operation-family name, operand and result schemas, evidence, behavior metadata, ownership/initialization transition and failure obligations, plus an explicit unqualified target/implementation marker and zero admission flag. Behavior metadata combines effect hints with ownership, identity, borrowing and control classifications; it is not a K1 effect row. Some records name internal elaboration primitives and scope terminals and describe their source-level Result wrapping; they do not make internal operations source-callable. The separate bounded decoder checks the exact record sequence and bytes. Its expectation table and the producer table were generated from the same provisional transcription: the decoder is independently implemented, but this is not an independent semantic transcription from the specification. The names and the `PRIVATE-OVERLOAD-CANDIDATE1` label are proposals, not qualified or portable K1 operation IDs. Their `T`, Region, Nat and mode variables are schematic; this file does not expand or validate every concrete layout/mode case.

`K1OPS001` is the private registry. `K1POL002` length-prefixes the existing private base profile and this registry; its BLAKE3 digest is derived from exact bytes. `K1OMNF02` wraps that private policy, its digest and the existing source-checked adapter/raw-kernel candidate. The decoder validates both schemas and the digest, then rechecks the nested candidate against trusted expected source/package/module. Public eligibility stays false, and no digest or numeric ID alone grants artifact or capability authority.

The records are a bounded transcription of the listed K1 scalar and memory operation families, not a proven exhaustive expansion. The specification does not supply the final canonical operation-ID/overload registry or every explicit conversion/Result error schema; unresolved details are marked rather than invented. In particular, complete per-layout and per-mode overloads, target feasibility and helper implementations, full layout/refinement/dictionary semantics, full M12 schemas and error handling, source-adapter target/result-slot lowering, frame/resource/loan authority and real interface/implementation/manifest/certificate agreement are still missing. `abi_overload_gaps` keeps the eligibility gate closed. Independent semantic review against all normative chapters is required before expanding claims.

Focused O0/O2 tests reject every one-byte registry mutation and truncation, corrupted composite policy/candidate samples, bad source/package identities and truncated envelopes. They do not validate a complete compiler or replace C09/M13.
