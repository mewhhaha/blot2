# Private scalar/loop short-circuit candidate

This scratch candidate extends the source-owned typed mixed scalar/finite-loop arena to recognize Bool `&&` and `||` expressions with their specified precedence and structured Wasm short-circuit lowering. It replays the source and node types independently, retains the existing scalar and loop scope checks, and requires a separate decoded target match. Source type signatures are the bounded U32/Bool combinations of the poly-canonical fixture. Bool inputs are raw i32 and require a caller-supplied canonical 0 or 1.

The proof producer and independent consumer check short-circuit RHS under its actual source-derived predecessor, recheck exact source/alias/guard evidence, and prune literal-dead RHS only after full source/type checking. Joins intersect exact guard provenance; this remains a bounded, conservative fact system, not complete K1 reachability or fact propagation. Loop fact/backedge boundaries remain those of the existing mixed fixture. No full SSA, general effects/ownership, resource checking, host ABI, M13, complete C09 or public admission is claimed. The previous U32 scalar/loop byte/status matrix is sampled, not a performance or completeness claim.

Actual engine tests use target-rejected, valid-Wasm trap probes to confirm the emitted RHS runs only on the appropriate branch. Target mutations, strict truncations, source/type and node forgeries are checked in the bounded fixture tests. Independently retained immutable source and no concurrent mutation are required. An independent bounded-subset audit passed at O0 and O2; it does not establish general compiler completeness.
